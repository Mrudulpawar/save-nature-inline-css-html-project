Keep Our Earth Green

Nature is an important part of our life. Trees give us oxygen, fruits, shade and help to keep our environment clean. Water is also necessary for every living creature. Therefore, we should protect trees and use water carefully.

We can protect nature by planting more trees, saving water,
avoiding plastic and keeping our surroundings clean.
Small steps taken by everyone can make our Earth greener, cleaner and healthier for future generations.
